Leading a rally?

Your rally

Strength is relative: your rally is 1.00 unless you set a different scale, and a leader at 1.30 does 30% more damage per troop. Hits: 5 if you open within the first ~3.5 minutes, 4 if later. Your rally doesn't use a march queue.

Your buffs

Apply to your rally and every march you send. Leaders' own buffs are already part of their measured strength.

3 · Leaders you join

Fill in strengths with Measure leader strengths below after each bear. Untick Joining when a leader isn't playing this event. Tick Full early if their rally is full before your rotating marches can get in. Min and Cap override the joiner minimum and cap for that leader. Rotating marches skip any rally whose minimum is above their size.

Measure leader strengths

After a bear, paste the presets you used (same format as Copy presets) and your hits from the damage list, one per line, like "Leader 1 5,866,987". Hits from your own rally go under your name. Each hit is compared with what that preset should do, so different preset sizes and mixes are accounted for.

4 · How rallies launch

Launch pattern

Staggered: rallies open a few seconds apart, so a free march can join whichever rally leaves next. Max hits per rotating march depends on how close to launch you can still get in: about 6.5 when you can only join 3–4 minutes out, about 10 in the last minute. All at once: every rally opens and launches together, so each march joins one leader every round.

Joiner march size

Applies to every leader unless their Min or Cap says otherwise.

Advanced: your troop stats

Used to compare troop types (which matters when a cap is hit) and when measuring leader strengths. Enter the totals from your stats screen.
